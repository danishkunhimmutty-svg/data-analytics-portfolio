# Tata — Data Visualization: Empowering Business with Effective Insights

A Forage job simulation completed as a consultant for an online retail 
store, analyzing sales data to inform an executive expansion strategy.

## Business Context
The CEO and CMO wanted insights on revenue drivers, seasonal trends, 
top-performing markets, and key customers to guide a business expansion 
decision.

## My Process
1. **Framed the business scenario** — drafted 8 questions (4 for CEO, 
   4 for CMO) anticipating what each leader would want to know, from 
   an operations vs. marketing lens.
2. **Chose the right visuals** — matched each executive request to the 
   chart type that would communicate it most clearly (trend, ranking, 
   comparison, geographic).
3. **Cleaned the data** — removed returns recorded as negative 
   quantities and rows with invalid (≤$0) unit prices before analysis.
4. **Built the visuals in Power BI** — four dashboards answering:
   - Q1: Monthly revenue trend for 2011
   - Q2: Top 10 countries by revenue & quantity (excl. UK)
   - Q3: Top 10 customers by revenue, ranked
   - Q4: Product demand by country in a single view (excl. UK)
5. **Presented findings** — recorded a video walkthrough tying each 
   visual back to a concrete recommendation for the CEO/CMO.

## Key Insights
- [September have peak level revenue]
- [ top 2-3 countries by revenue outside the UK are Netherlands , France ,germen]
- [Netherlands ,EIRE,FRANCE regions showed the strongest demand for expansion]
