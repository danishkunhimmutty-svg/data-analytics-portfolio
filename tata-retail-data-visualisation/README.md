# Tata — Data Visualization: Empowering Business with Effective Insights

A Forage job simulation completed as a consultant for an online retail store, analyzing sales data to provide insights for an executive expansion strategy.

## Business Context

The CEO and CMO wanted insights into revenue drivers, seasonal trends, top-performing markets, and key customers to support a business expansion decision.

## My Process

- Framed the business scenario by preparing 8 questions — 4 for the CEO and 4 for the CMO.
- Matched each business question with an appropriate visualization.
- Cleaned the dataset by removing returns recorded as negative quantities.
- Removed rows containing invalid unit prices (≤ $0).
- Built interactive Power BI visualizations and dashboards.
- Presented the findings through a recorded video walkthrough connecting the analysis to business recommendations.

## Power BI Analysis

### Q1 — Monthly Revenue Trend
Analyzed monthly revenue trends for 2011 to identify seasonal patterns and peak revenue periods.

**Key finding:** September recorded the peak revenue level.

### Q2 — Top 10 Countries by Revenue and Quantity
Identified the top-performing countries outside the UK based on revenue and quantity.

**Key finding:** The Netherlands, France, and Germany were among the strongest markets outside the UK.

### Q3 — Top 10 Customers by Revenue
Ranked customers according to their total revenue contribution to identify the most valuable customers.

### Q4 — Product Demand by Country
Analyzed product demand across countries in a single Power BI view, excluding the UK.

**Key finding:** The Netherlands, EIRE, and France showed particularly strong demand and potential for expansion.

## Recommendation

Based on the analysis, the business should prioritize markets demonstrating strong revenue and product demand, particularly the Netherlands, EIRE, and France, while continuing to monitor customer value and seasonal revenue patterns.
