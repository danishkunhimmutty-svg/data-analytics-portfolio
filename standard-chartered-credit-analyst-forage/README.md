Standard Chartered – Credit Analyst Job Simulation (Forage)
This repository contains my work from the Standard Chartered Credit Analyst virtual job simulation on Forage, completed as part of my ongoing data/finance analytics upskilling.
Scenario
Acting as a Credit Analyst on Standard Chartered's Banking & Coverage team, I carried out the annual credit review of Green Solutions Manufacturing Ltd — a fictional long-standing client and market-leading manufacturer of sustainable drinkware (reusable water bottles and coffee cups), seeking additional credit to fund further sustainability-related capital investment.
Task 1: Financial Health Analysis
Analysed two years (FY2022–FY2023) of the client's Income Statement, Balance Sheet, and Cash Flow Statement
Calculated and interpreted key credit metrics: profitability (EBITDA margin, operating margin), liquidity (current ratio), and leverage (Debt/EBITDA, net leverage, interest cover)
Produced a preliminary report highlighting the client's financial position, key risks, and actionable recommendations for the credit team
Key findings: Revenue grew 71% YoY with EBITDA margin more than doubling (4.7% → 10.3%). Leverage fell sharply from 7.8x to 1.2x Debt/EBITDA, and interest cover improved from 2.1x to 17.5x — a materially strengthening credit profile. The main risk flagged was a declining current ratio (1.87x → 1.37x), worth ongoing monitoring.
Task 2: Industry Research & Presentation
Conducted industry research into the reusable water bottle and reusable coffee cup markets, including market size, growth trends, and competitive landscape
Layered in macroeconomic context (UK interest rates, raw material/steel cost pressures, and incoming UK/EU packaging regulation) to assess the client's broader creditworthiness
Combined quantitative and qualitative findings into a presentation script recommending the bank proceed with the credit request, with liquidity and input-cost exposure flagged as monitoring points
Skills Demonstrated
Financial statement analysis and credit risk assessment
Key accounting ratio calculation and interpretation (Excel)
Industry and macroeconomic research
Written and presentation-style business communication
